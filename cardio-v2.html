Heart Rate Calculation
Tanaka Formula (most accurate for active adults):
MHR = 208 − (0.7 × Age) = 208 − (0.7 × 28) = 192 BPM
Karvonen Method — Heart Rate Reserve targets use resting HR (RHR):
Target HR = RHR + (HRR × Zone%)  |  HRR = MHR − RHR
Zones below calculated using pure % of MHR (Tanaka). If you know your resting HR, the Karvonen method gives tighter personalized zones — subtract your RHR from 192 to get HRR, then apply zone percentages to HRR and add RHR back.

Example with RHR 55: HRR = 137. Zone 4 target = 55 + (137 × 0.80) = 165 bpm.

What Happens in Your Body — Week by Week
WK
1–2
Neural & Metabolic Priming
Your body learns pacing. Lactate buffering improves slightly. You'll feel it's harder than it looks — that's normal. Stroke volume changes haven't begun yet. HR will feel very high for the perceived effort.
WK
3–4
Cardiac Output Increases
Left ventricular volume begins expanding (cardiac hypertrophy — the athletic kind). Stroke volume increases. You'll notice the same interval pace feels slightly easier. Resting HR may drop 1–3 bpm.
WK
5–6
Mitochondrial Density + VO₂ Gains
Mitochondria proliferate in muscle cells. Oxygen extraction improves. VO₂ max measurably increases — often 5–8% in untrained or lapsed athletes. Lactate threshold shifts right.
WK
7–8
Full Protocol Adaptation
You'll likely need to increase pace/resistance to maintain Zone 4–5 HR — a sign VO₂ max has improved. Running economy improves. Time to progress (longer intervals, more sets, or move to 4×4 protocol).
The Science — Why VO₂ Max = Biological Age
🫀
Cardiac Remodeling
HIIT induces eccentric left ventricular hypertrophy — the heart literally gets bigger and pumps more per beat. Stroke volume can increase 15–25% with consistent training, lowering resting HR and improving cardiac efficiency at all intensities.
⚗️
Mitochondrial Biogenesis
Zone 4–5 work activates PGC-1α, the master regulator of mitochondrial production. More mitochondria = more ATP production = higher sustainable power output. This is the primary mechanism of VO₂ max gain at the cellular level.
📉
Longevity Data
Peter Attia and Levine et al. (2018) JAMA study: each MET (~3.5 ml/kg/min VO₂) increase in fitness reduces mortality risk ~13%. Going from "low fit" to "moderate fit" cuts 10-year mortality risk more than quitting smoking.
🧬
Telomere Effect
HIIT has been shown to increase telomerase activity — the enzyme that protects and rebuilds telomere length. Telomere length is a direct biological age marker. 2019 Mayo Clinic study: HIIT increased telomerase activity ~69% vs. resistance training's 28%.
Peri-Workout Nutrition
60–90 min before
Pre-Workout
Light, fast-digesting carbs + small protein. Oats + banana, rice cake + nut butter, or Greek yogurt + fruit. Avoid fat + fiber — slows gastric emptying, kills high-intensity output. Hydrate 500ml water.
During
During Session
20 minutes — no food needed. Water only. Electrolytes optional if sweating heavily (sodium, potassium). No sugar drinks during HIIT — blunts the fat-burning and mitochondrial signaling stimulus.
30–60 min after
Post-Workout
Protein + carbs within the anabolic window. 30–40g protein + 40–60g carbs. Chicken + rice, protein shake + banana, eggs + toast. Prioritizes glycogen replenishment and muscle protein synthesis for the cardiac adaptations.
